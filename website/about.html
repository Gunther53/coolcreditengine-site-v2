About

Building infrastructure for emissions data that holds up.

CoolCreditEngine was created to bridge a gap: organizations are being asked to produce increasingly precise emissions data, but the underlying systems often look nothing like the ones used for financial data.

Perspective

The project reflects experience in financial systems, compliance architectures, and emissions work. The focus is on making emissions data usable, explainable, and durable enough to support the decisions that depend on it.

What this is and isn’t

  • It is an engine for structuring and governing emissions data.
  • It is not a marketing‑heavy ESG dashboard or offset marketplace.
  • It is designed to sit quietly behind the scenes and make other tools stronger.

Long‑term view

Over time, CoolCreditEngine aims to become a reference point for how to treat emissions data: modular, inspectable, and capable of supporting assurance without being brittle.

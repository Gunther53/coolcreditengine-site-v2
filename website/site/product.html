Product overview

A modular platform for emissions accounting.

CoolCreditEngine is designed as a set of interoperable modules that can be combined to support different regulatory environments, industries, and data maturities.

Core modules

  • Signals layer: Ingests operational and supplier data from systems you already use.
  • Rules layer: Applies deterministic emissions logic and factors chosen by your organization.
  • Ledgered views: Produces structured outputs that can be versioned and audited over time.
  • Registry interface (optional): Bridges to external registries and credit systems where relevant.

Designed for change

Regulations, factors, and reporting standards will continue to evolve. CoolCreditEngine is built to keep those elements modular, so organizations can adapt without rebuilding their entire stack.

  • Configurable rules instead of hard‑wiring assumptions
  • Separation between raw data, logic, and outward‑facing reports
  • Clear boundaries between internal accounting and external instruments like credits

Who this is for

The platform is aimed at organizations that need more than a dashboard: they need a backbone that can underpin reporting, customer disclosures, and internal decision‑making.

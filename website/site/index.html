What CoolCreditEngine focuses on

CoolCreditEngine is a modular platform for organizations that need to treat emissions like a governed data product: consistent, explainable, and ready to support reporting and assurance.

  • Emissions tracking aligned with operational activity
  • Procurement‑level insight into supplier and material footprints
  • Compliance‑ready views that can support CSRD, “Fit for 55”, and OEM requirements
  • Optional integration paths for registries and credit issuance rails

Key pillars

  • Data in: Operational and document‑based signals brought into a consistent model
  • Logic: Deterministic rules and factors that can be inspected and governed
  • Outputs: Emissions views that can be shared with auditors, customers, and regulators
  • Extensibility: Modular components that can be adapted to evolving regulatory scope

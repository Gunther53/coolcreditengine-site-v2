Compliance & readiness

Emissions data built for scrutiny.

CoolCreditEngine is designed to help organizations organize their emissions data in a way that supports reporting under frameworks such as CSRD, “Fit for 55” policies, and OEM supply‑chain expectations.

Compliance‑ready views

  • Structured outputs that can underpin CSRD‑aligned reports
  • Support for supplier and material‑level views to satisfy OEM requirements
  • Consistent handling of updates and restatements when assumptions change

Role in your stack

CoolCreditEngine is not a replacement for every ESG or reporting tool you use. It is focused on the accounting layer that sits beneath dashboards and disclosures.

  • Upstream of ESG dashboards and sustainability reporting tools
  • Downstream of ERP, procurement, and operational systems
  • Designed to feed, not fragment, your existing workflows

Prepared for assurance

As assurance expectations increase, organizations will need emissions records that can withstand questions from auditors, customers, and regulators. CoolCreditEngine is built with that path in mind.

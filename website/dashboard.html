Operational view

A focused dashboard for emissions operations.

The CoolCreditEngine dashboard is designed for internal users who need to understand what the system is seeing, not for public storytelling. It surfaces the information required to manage emissions data as an asset.

Key perspectives

  • Emissions tracking: Views aligned with facilities, assets, or supplier categories.
  • Procurement insights: Signals tied to materials, vendors, and contract types.
  • Data health: High‑level indicators to show where inputs are incomplete or out of date.

For internal teams

The dashboard is built for the teams who need to manage emissions as part of their day‑to‑day work: operations, procurement, finance, and sustainability.

  • Helps prioritize where to improve data quality
  • Supports conversations with suppliers and internal stakeholders
  • Provides a shared view without exposing every technical detail
